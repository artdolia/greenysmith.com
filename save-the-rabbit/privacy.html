Privacy Policy

Save the Rabbit: Farm Escape is developed and published by Greeny Smith LLC.

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Information We Collect

Save the Rabbit: Farm Escape does not require you to create an account and Greeny Smith LLC does not directly collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or other personal information through the game.

The app uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information necessary to provide advertising, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, diagnose technical issues, and operate their services.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements that you may choose to watch in exchange for an in-game benefit.

Google Mobile Ads may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising data, user interactions with advertisements, diagnostic information, and performance information. The exact information processed depends on your device settings, region, consent choices, and Google's services.

For more information about how Google handles information, see Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising Consent and Tracking

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage consent for advertising-related data processing.

On iOS, the app may also request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency system. You may allow or deny this permission. Denying tracking permission does not prevent you from using the game.

You can also change tracking permissions later through the privacy settings on your device.

In-App Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases, including consumable game items and an option to remove advertisements.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Greeny Smith LLC does not receive or store your payment card information. Apple's handling of payment and account information is governed by Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

Game Data

The app may store gameplay information and purchase-related state locally on your device, such as game progress, settings, available power-ups, and whether the Remove Ads purchase has been unlocked.

This local gameplay information is used to operate the game and is not used by Greeny Smith LLC to identify you personally.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience. Greeny Smith LLC does not knowingly request or directly collect personal information from children through the game.

Third-Party Services

The app currently uses services provided by Apple and Google, including the Apple App Store, StoreKit, Google AdMob, and Google's User Messaging Platform. These companies may process information under their own privacy policies.

Your Choices

You can control advertising consent where available through consent messages presented by the app. You can manage Apple's tracking permission through your device settings.

You may also choose not to watch rewarded advertisements and may purchase the optional Remove Ads feature where offered.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app, applicable laws, or the services used by the app change. The current version will always be posted on this page.
